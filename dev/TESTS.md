# What the tests cover

Generated by `dev/tests-md.php` from the last `dev/dev.sh ci` run; one line per assertion, grouped by the heading the suite printed. Do not edit by hand.

## Smoke (console, `dev/smoke.php`)

### Settings

- the seeded settings: News and Clothing on by default
- config/slug-equals-title.php overrides the category groups of the stored settings
- an empty checkbox list, the ";" placeholder of 3.x settings and null normalize to lists of handles
- entries, categories and products are the supported element types
- the settings page lists sections, category groups and product types
- with the sections of the site

### Defaults per type

- a new News entry gets its slug from the title
- a changed title changes the slug
- a slug set by hand is overwritten while the entry follows its title
- a Pages entry (off by default) keeps the slug it was given
- and keeps it when its title changes
- isEnabled() follows the defaults of the types while nothing is remembered
- console saves remember nothing

### Remembered choice

- an entry switched on follows its title although its section is off
- an entry switched off keeps its own slug although its section is on
- one row per element: the latest choice replaces the earlier one
- switched on again, the entry follows its title again
- isEnabled() reads the remembered choice

### Resaving

- php craft resave/entries brings a stale slug back in line with the title

### Uniqueness and normalization

- two entries with the same title get unique slugs and URIs
- punctuation is dropped like Craft does for new entries

### Sites

- the English site transliterates with Craft's default map
- the German site transliterates the German way
- the English slug is untouched by the German save

### Drafts and revisions

- a draft takes the remembered choice of its canonical entry
- a draft follows its title
- the draft's own choice wins over the canonical entry's, which keeps its own
- applying the draft saves the canonical entry with its own choice: the slug follows the title
- the draft's row went with the draft
- saves create revisions; the plugin leaves them alone

### Empty titles

- an empty title leaves the slug alone

### Nested entries

- nested entries (Matrix blocks) have no section: not supported, never followed
- the page keeps its slug

### Categories

- a category of a group that is on follows its title
- and when its title changes
- a category of a group that is off keeps its slug

### Products

- a product of a type that is on follows its title
- switched off, a product keeps its slug

### Migration from 3.x

- the 3.x table is folded into the new one and dropped
- of several rows of an element, the latest choice wins
- a single row migrates; a choice already in the new table is kept over the old one
- rows of elements that are gone are left behind
- the migrated choice is in effect: the entry keeps its slug
- a dump that lower-cased the table name (MySQL on macOS or Windows, #21) migrates too
- the migration cannot be reverted

### Install

- installing over an existing table is a no-op
- uninstalling drops the table
- and installing creates it again
- the 3.x migration creates the table when it is missing

## Control panel in Chrome (`dev/ui.mts`)

### The switch in the slug field

- a News entry has the "Slug equals title" switch in the slug field, next to the input
- on: the slug input is replaced by "Overwritten by title"
- saved: the slug equals the title, the switch stays on

### Switching off keeps a slug of your own

- switched off, the slug input is back and free
- the title no longer touches the slug
- saved: the slug of your own is kept, the switch stays off
- switched on again, "Overwritten by title" takes the input's place
- saved: the slug follows the title again

### A section that is off

- a Pages entry has the switch off and its slug free
- the title leaves the slug alone
- the demo page "About us" keeps its slug "about"

### A type without a slug field

- the Home single (its entry type hides the slug field) shows no switch
- a category of the Topics group has the switch in its slug field, on
- a product of the Clothing type has it too, on

### The slideout editor

- the slideout has the switch in its slug field
- switched on in the slideout: "Overwritten by title" takes the input's place
- saved from the slideout: the slug equals the title, the switch is on

### The German site

- the saved slug is transliterated the German way

### The settings page

- Sections: News is ticked, Pages is not
- Category Groups: Topics is ticked but read-only, with the note about config/slug-equals-title.php
- ticking Pages and saving keeps it ticked
- the page entry already switched on (remembered) is still on
- unticked again
