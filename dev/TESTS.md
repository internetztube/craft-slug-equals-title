# What the tests cover

Generated by `dev/tests-md.php` from the last `dev/dev.sh ci` run; one line per assertion, grouped by the heading the suite printed. Do not edit by hand.

## Smoke (console, `dev/smoke.php`)

### Settings

- the seeded settings: News on by default, stored by UID
- an empty checkbox list ("" or null) normalizes to an empty list
- the ";" placeholder of settings saved before 3.1 and non-strings are dropped
- the Pages section has two entry types
- other element types (assets, users) have no setting and are never followed

### Defaults per type

- a new News entry gets its slug from the title
- a changed title changes the slug
- a slug set by hand is overwritten while the entry follows its title
- a Pages entry (off by default) keeps the slug it was given
- and keeps it when its title changes
- isEnabled() follows the defaults of the types while nothing is remembered
- console saves remember nothing

### Stop words

- the stop words of the site language fall out of the title: whole words, any case
- a title of nothing but stop words keeps every word
- the words are per language: de-AT and de-CH use those of "de"
- inside a word nothing is dropped, and a language without words keeps the title
- a News entry gets its slug without them
- without words, every word stays
- the settings page posts a comma-separated line per language, the config file lists: one line of trimmed, lower-cased, unique words each; languages without words are dropped, a locale key (en-GB) counts as its language
- the settings page gets a line per language of the sites: en (for en-US), de

### Entry types and handles

- an entry matches its section and its entry type, by UID and by handle
- a single entry type of a section can be on while the rest of the section is off
- and its entries follow their title
- handles work too

### Remembered choice

- an entry switched on follows its title although its section is off
- an entry switched off keeps its own slug although its section is on
- one row per element: the latest choice replaces the earlier one
- switched on again, the entry follows its title again
- isEnabled() reads the remembered choice

### Resaving

- php artisan craft:resave:entries brings a stale slug back in line with the title

### Uniqueness and normalization

- two entries with the same title get unique slugs and URIs
- punctuation is dropped like Craft does for new entries

### Sites

- the English site transliterates with Craft's default map
- the German site transliterates the German way
- the English slug is untouched by the German save

### Drafts and revisions

- a draft takes the remembered choice of its canonical entry
- a draft follows its title
- the draft's own choice wins over the canonical entry's, which keeps its own
- applying the draft saves the canonical entry with its own choice: the slug follows the title
- the draft's row went with the draft
- saves create revisions; the plugin leaves them alone

### Empty titles

- an empty title leaves the slug alone

### Nested entries

- nested entries (Matrix blocks) have no section: not supported, never followed
- the page keeps its slug

### Install

- uninstalling drops the table
- and installing creates it again

## Control panel over HTTP (`dev/e2e.php`)

### Fixtures

- a News entry (on by default) and a Pages entry (off) with its own slug

### Login

- the login page gives a session and a CSRF cookie
- login succeeds

### Edit page

- the News entry's edit page renders
- its sidebar form has the "Slug equals title" lightswitch
- right after the slug field
- on, editable
- the Pages entry's edit page has the switch off
- the German site's edit page has the switch too
- the single's edit page has no slug field (its entry type hides it) and so no switch
- a revision shows the switch read-only

### Saving

- saving the News entry with the switch on succeeds
- the slug follows the new title, whatever the slug input held
- and the choice is remembered
- the German site keeps its slug (titles are per site; it still has the old one); one row for the entry, not one per site
- with the switch off (the editor posts false), the slug typed into the form is kept
- a save without the switch in the request follows the remembered choice: off, so the slug stays
- the editor (a provisional draft, then applied) switches the entry on again
- an editor save without the switch in the request (unchanged, so not posted) follows the remembered choice: the slug follows the title
- the editor's direct save (elements/save with only the changed values, when nothing was autosaved before) follows it too
- switched off again
- the Pages entry switched on follows its title although its section is off
- a web save without the switch in the request follows the remembered choice: on, so the slug follows the title

### Drafts

- autosaving a draft with the switch on succeeds
- the draft's slug follows the draft's title
- the draft remembers its own choice
- the canonical entry keeps its own
- publishing the draft succeeds
- the canonical entry has the draft's title, a slug from it, and the switch on
- the editor's provisional draft of an entry without a remembered choice follows the title
- and applying it gives the entry that slug
- the editor of an element the plugin does not support (the user account) has no switch

### Slideout

- the slideout screen renders
- its sidebar has the switch (on: remembered) with its input, namespaced like the rest of the slideout
- and the script that moves it into the slug field comes with the screen
- a Matrix block's slideout (a nested entry, no section) has no switch
- a Matrix block's own edit page (Craft's generic element editor) has no switch either

### Settings page

- the settings page renders
- Sections are listed by UID
- the entry types of Pages (two of them) are listed under it, by "section:entryType" UIDs; News with one entry type lists none
- News is ticked, Pages not
- with the config file in place, Sections is read-only with a note
- News and Landing page (named by handle in the file) are ticked, Pages (by UID) is not
- the stop words from the file are read-only with a note
- a Landing page entry follows its title: the file turned its entry type on
- Stop words: a line per site language (en, de), empty
- saving the settings succeeds
- the ticked section and entry type are stored by UID
- the stop words are stored per language as one line of lower-cased words, shown again as it is; a language left empty is not stored
- and they are in effect: an entry saved with the switch on gets its slug without them
- the seeded settings are back, no stop words

- the demo page "About us" keeps its slug "about"

- the saved slug is transliterated the German way
