# What the tests cover

Generated by `dev/tests-md.php` from the last `dev/dev.sh ci` run; one line per assertion, grouped by the heading the suite printed. Do not edit by hand.

## Smoke (console, `dev/smoke.php`)

### Settings

- the seeded settings: News on by default, stored by UID
- config/craft/slug-equals-title.php sets the category groups (by handle), over whatever the stored settings hold
- an empty checkbox list, the ";" placeholder of old settings and null normalize to lists of handles
- entries and categories (Yii2 adapter) are the supported element types
- the settings page lists sections and category groups
- with the sections of the site
- the Pages section has two entry types
- other element types (assets, users) have no setting and are never followed

### Defaults per type

- a new News entry gets its slug from the title
- a changed title changes the slug
- a slug set by hand is overwritten while the entry follows its title
- a Pages entry (off by default) keeps the slug it was given
- and keeps it when its title changes
- isEnabled() follows the defaults of the types while nothing is remembered
- console saves remember nothing

### Entry types and handles

- an entry matches its section and its entry type, by UID and by handle
- a single entry type of a section can be on while the rest of the section is off
- and its entries follow their title
- handles work too

### Remembered choice

- an entry switched on follows its title although its section is off
- an entry switched off keeps its own slug although its section is on
- one row per element: the latest choice replaces the earlier one
- switched on again, the entry follows its title again
- isEnabled() reads the remembered choice

### Resaving

- php artisan craft:resave:entries brings a stale slug back in line with the title

### Uniqueness and normalization

- two entries with the same title get unique slugs and URIs
- punctuation is dropped like Craft does for new entries

### Sites

- the English site transliterates with Craft's default map
- the German site transliterates the German way
- the English slug is untouched by the German save

### Drafts and revisions

- a draft takes the remembered choice of its canonical entry
- a draft follows its title
- the draft's own choice wins over the canonical entry's, which keeps its own
- applying the draft saves the canonical entry with its own choice: the slug follows the title
- the draft's row went with the draft
- saves create revisions; the plugin leaves them alone

### Empty titles

- an empty title leaves the slug alone

### Nested entries

- nested entries (Matrix blocks) have no section: not supported, never followed
- the page keeps its slug

### Categories (Yii2 adapter)

- a category of a group that is on follows its title
- and when its title changes
- a category of a group that is off keeps its slug

### Install

- uninstalling drops the table
- and installing creates it again
